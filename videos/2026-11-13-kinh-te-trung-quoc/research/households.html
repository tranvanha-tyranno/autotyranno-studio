Households’ Income and Consumption Expenditure in the First Three Quarters of 2026

2026-10-20 10:00 Print| Large| Medium| Small

I. Households’ Income

 

In the first three quarters of 2026, the nationwide per capita disposable income was 34,230 yuan, a nominal increase of 5.3% over the same period of the previous year, and a real increase of 4.4% after adjusting for price factors. In terms of urban and rural areas, the per capita disposable income of urban residents was 44,945 yuan, an increase of 4.5% (unless otherwise specified, all growth rates mentioned are year-on-year nominal growth rates), and a real increase of 3.6% after adjusting for price factors; the per capita disposable income of rural residents was 18,809 yuan, an increase of 6.3%, and a real increase of 5.6% after adjusting for price factors.

 

By source of income, in the first three quarters of the year, the per capita income from wages and salaries of residents nationwide was 19,653 yuan, an increase of 5.3%, accounting for 57.4% of the per capita disposable income; the per capita net business income was 5,542 yuan, an increase of 6.6%, accounting for 16.2% of the per capita disposable income; the per capita net income from properties was 2,671 yuan, an increase of 1.6%, accounting for 7.8% of the per capita disposable income; the per capita net income from transfer was 6,364 yuan, an increase of 5.7%, accounting for 18.6% of the per capita disposable income.

 

In the first three quarters of 2026, the median of the nationwide per capita disposable income was 28,388 yuan, an increase of 4.6%, and the median was 82.9% of the average. Among them, the median of the per capita disposable income of urban residents was 39,728 yuan, an increase of 3.9%, and the median was 88.4% of the average; the median of the per capita disposable income of rural residents was 15,895 yuan, an increase of 5.7%, and the median was 84.5% of the average.

II. Consumption Expenditure of Households

 

In the first three quarters of 2026, the nationwide per capita consumption expenditure was 22,354 yuan, a nominal increase of 3.6% over the same period of the previous year, and a real increase of 2.7% after adjusting for price factors. In terms of urban and rural areas, the per capita consumption expenditure of urban residents was 27,312 yuan, an increase of 3.0%, and a real increase of 2.1% after adjusting for price factors; the per capita consumption expenditure of rural residents was 15,218 yuan, an increase of 4.3%, and a real increase of 3.5% after adjusting for price factors.

 

In the first three quarters of 2026, the nationwide per capita consumption expenditure on food, tobacco and liquor was 6,643 yuan, an increase of 4.5%, accounting for 29.7% of the per capita consumption expenditure; the per capita consumption expenditure on clothing was 1,172 yuan, an increase of 4.1%, accounting for 5.2% of the per capita consumption expenditure; the per capita consumption expenditure on residence was 4,753 yuan, an increase of 1.9%, accounting for 21.3% of the per capita consumption expenditure; the per capita consumption expenditure on household facilities, articles and services was 1,281 yuan, an increase of 4.7%, accounting for 5.7% of the per capita consumption expenditure; the per capita consumption expenditure on transportation and telecommunication was 3,190 yuan, an increase of 2.8%, accounting for 14.3% of per capita consumption expenditure; the per capita consumption expenditure on education, culture and recreation was 2,647 yuan, an increase of 4.6%, accounting for 11.8% of the per capita consumption expenditure; the per capita consumption expenditure on health care and medical services was 1,974 yuan, an increase of 2.3%, accounting for 8.8% of the per capita consumption expenditure; the per capita consumption expenditure on miscellaneous goods and services was 694 yuan, an increase of 8.7%, accounting for 3.1% of the per capita consumption expenditure.

Influenced by urbanization, population migration and other factors, the composition of the population has changed over time by urban-rural area and by region, which sometimes leads to the phenomenon that the growth rate of some income and consumption expenditure items nationwide exceeds the growth range of corresponding income and consumption expenditure items of urban and rural residents. Especially in the process of urbanization, a segment of the rural population with relatively high incomes moves to urban areas, but they often belong to lower-income groups in urban settings. Their migration exerts a downward effect on the income and consumption expenditure of both urban and rural residents. Nonetheless, whether in urban or rural areas, the growth effect of this segment is reflected in the overall income and expenditure growth of residents nationwide.

However, the measure is vulnerable to the influence of extreme values. The median reflects the income of the surveyed household at the midpoint of the distribution, which is relatively stable and free from the influence of extreme values. However, it cannot reflect the overall structure.

Annotations:

 

1. Notes on Indicators

 

Disposable income of residents refers to the sum of final consumption expenditure and savings that residents can use, that is, the income that residents can use for free disposal, including both cash income and physical income. According to the source of income, disposable income includes income from wages and salaries, net business income, net income from properties and net income from transfer.

 

Consumption expenditure of residents refers to all the expenditure that residents use to meet their daily needs, including both cash and physical consumption. Consumption expenditure includes eight categories: food, tobacco and liquor, clothing, residence, household facilities, articles and services, transportation and telecommunication, education, culture and recreation, health care and medical services, and miscellaneous goods and services.

 

The median of the nationwide per capita disposable income refers to the per capita disposable income of residents that lies at the midpoint of all surveyed households ranked from low to high on the basis of per capita disposable income.

 

The quarterly income and consumption expenditure data do not cover the income and consumption of the self-produced and self-used parts of residents, which are included in the annual income and consumption expenditure data.

 

2. Survey Methods

 

The nationwide, urban and rural residents’ income and consumption expenditure data come from the household income and consumption expenditure and living condition surveys organized and implemented by the National Bureau of Statistics of China (NBS of China), which are released quarterly.

 

Using a stratified, multi-stage, probability sampling method proportional to the size of the population, the NBS of China randomly selects 160 thousand households in nearly 2,000 counties (cities and districts) from 31 provinces (autonomous regions and municipalities) as surveyed households.

 

According to the unified rules and methods, the directly-affiliated survey teams stationed in localities by the NBS of China organize the surveyed households to keep accounts of data on residents’ income, expenditure, family business operations, production and investment; meanwhile, according to the unified questionnaire, the survey teams collect other information such as household members and the employment status of the labor force, the ownership of housing and durable consumer goods, and the coverage of residents’ basic social and public services. After the completion of data collection, the survey teams at the municipal and county level use unified methods and data processing procedures to code, review and input the original investigation information, and then directly transmit the basic household data to the NBS of China for centralized summary and calculation.

 

3. Additional Notes

 

Due to rounding, the sub-items may not add up to the aggregate totals.
