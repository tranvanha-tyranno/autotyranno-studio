# Physical finance explainer / preview branch

New optional content-first animation branch after the user rejected the earlier minimal film as slides. The old Minimal composition is retained unchanged.

`FlowFilm` accepts the existing `FinancePlan`. Audio and captions remain in the host wrapper. All objects are original SVG/React parts; there are no generated visual assets, fetched fonts or provider calls. `timing.ts` reads exact-take `plan.words` in seconds, converts to 30fps and locates actions against the corresponding spoken phrase inside each segment. Fallback positions support missing alignment; they are not presented as exact-take timings.

The daily and meal scenes share one printer/calculator and receipt pile. The date tape has exactly 30 numbered cells, moves into the input once, prints 30 individual 60,000 receipts and stops at 1,800,000. The meal adds a distinct 200,000 receipt to the same machine. Its 2,000,000 output is matched to the raise envelope, whose two one-million tokens leave and whose remaining amount becomes zero. The repeated objects serve a cumulative calculation rather than decorative ambience.

The before/after model conserves illustrated million-value tokens: 12 becomes 10 spent plus 2 retained; the raise adds 2, and the extra expense removes those 2 while the retained amount stays 2. The transfer scene uses a single paper length divided into differently sized parts to represent personally chosen allocations; these lengths are explicitly symbolic, not recommended percentages or extra numeric claims. Essential costs and debt receive their allocation at the actual narration cues before the optional savings transfer.

Render proof and actual motion still require inspection. Successful type checking is not evidence of aesthetic or audiovisual acceptance.
